Booking catering

How do I place a catering order?

Call (925) 691-3818 and tell us what you'd like, how many people you're feeding, and when you need it. We'll confirm the total and pickup time.

How much notice do you need?

The more the better. For anything over a few trays, or for holiday tamale orders, call at least 2-3 days ahead so we can prep enough.

Do you deliver catering orders?

Pickup is standard for catering trays. Ask when you call if you need delivery for a larger event.
